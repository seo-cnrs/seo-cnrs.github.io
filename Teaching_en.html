Teaching at the University of Strasbourg, ENSIIE and TPS

  • Interaction Engineering and Virtual Reality (Master ISI, University of Strasbourg): in my module “Virtual Human Modeling”, 6 h of lectures are given, introducing techniques for modeling and animating virtual humans: body, face and garments.
  • Medical Robotics Intensive Course (Master's and doctoral students from Paris, Strasbourg and the United States): in my module “Geometric Modeling and Virtual Reality”, 4 h of lectures are given, covering the graphics system, polygonal mesh representation, collision detection, data structures for managing 3D space, and real-time rendering techniques for virtual reality.
  • Disciplinary English (L3, University of Strasbourg): each semester, 4 h of tutorials are given on the history and the fundamentals of computer graphics. The aim is to strengthen the ability to read and present scientific articles in English.
  • Geometry and Image Synthesis I (Master, Télécom Physique Strasbourg): an introductory course in computer graphics. The successive stages of the graphics pipeline are taught: basic geometric modeling, transformations, projection, clipping, illumination, texture mapping, hidden-surface determination, antialiasing and rasterization.
  • Geometry and Image Synthesis II (Master, Télécom Physique Strasbourg): an advanced course in computer graphics. Geometric modeling by means of hierarchical models, and optimization and space-subdivision methods for real-time rendering.
  • Compiler Construction (1st-year engineering, ENSIIE): an introduction to the basic techniques of compiler design. Syntactic and semantic analysis (lexical analysis, parsing and parsers), instruction selection, control-flow graphs, making calling conventions explicit, and register allocation.
  • Software Engineering (M1, University of Strasbourg): 26.25 HED of lectures and practical sessions on project management, version control, programming and documentation tools, system planning, and related topics.
  • 3D Modeling and Simulation (Master IRIV, Télécom Physique Strasbourg): in my module “Acquisition and Reconstruction of Deformation”, approximately 5 h of lectures and 2 h of practical sessions are given on surface representation, deformation and geometric modeling. Since 2022, facial modeling and body modeling have been covered.
    • Building geometric models
    • Affine transformation
    • Cloth simulation
    • Deformation methods I
    • Deformation methods II
  • Algorithms and Programming 2 (L1, University of Strasbourg): this course is normally divided into 3 groups, taught by 4 or 5 instructors. One of these groups was taken in charge for 22 h of practical sessions in 2016/2017 and 19 h of integrated courses in 2017/2018 and 2018/2019, on programming in the C language.

Teaching at Chungnam National University

  • Algorithms and Programming, levels I and II: an introduction to programming languages (I) and advanced programming (II), with practical work in the C language.
  • Computer Graphics: an introductory course on programming for interactive 3D computer graphics. The course comprises a theoretical part and practical sessions with OpenGL.
  • Data Structures and Programming I: abstract data types and data representation are taught: queues, stacks, linked lists, graphs and binary trees. The basic algorithms are studied together with their time and space complexity.
  • Virtual Reality: an introduction to virtual reality technologies and their applications. Topics covered: input and output devices, system architecture with an in-depth study of devices for real-time rendering, programming, human-factor considerations, and emerging applications.
  • Advanced Computer Graphics: presentation and discussion of advanced topics in 3D computer graphics: scalable graphics, graphics-pipeline optimization, 3D scene management, skinning, and collision detection.
  • Computer Animation: an introduction to 3D computer graphics and computer animation. Topics: representation and transformation of graphics objects, 3D deformation techniques, data capture techniques, facial animation, character animation (keyframing, kinematics and inverse kinematics), cloth simulation, and behavioral animation.

Other Teaching Activities

  • Special courses for high-school students: a course on algorithmic reasoning for solving specific problems, together with programming practice, for students selected at the regional level.
  • Individual tutoring for high-school students: of the twenty students in the preceding “Special courses for high-school students” class, three were selected for the advanced level.
